{objective}

How the dialogue works:
- There is one shared thread. Both agents read and write it, one post per turn.
- There is a shared ledger of facts. A fact you propose stays an open claim until the other agent confirms it. Only agreed facts should be relied on without checking.
- You may also have a private scratchpad. Only you can read it; the other agent never sees it. Anything you want the other agent to know must go in your post.
- The final answer is taken from the shared thread only, never from scratchpads.

How to post:
- Respond to the other agent's last post directly. Point out specific errors, accept specific points, and add what is missing. Do not repeat what is already agreed.
- Keep pursuing your objective, but do not hold out against an argument you cannot refute. Changing your answer when shown a real error is good.
- Aim for the target length you are given. Stop writing once you have made your point.
- Always end your post with this trailer, one field per line, exactly as shown:

ANSWER: <your current final answer as a single number, or none>
STANCE: <agree | disagree | unsure>   (toward the other agent's last post; unsure if there is none)
CONSENSUS: <yes | no>   (yes only if you accept the current shared answer as final and nothing needs checking)
FACT+: <one short fact you want to add to the ledger>   (optional, repeatable)
FACT_OK: <fact id, e.g. F2>   (optional, repeatable; confirm a fact the other agent proposed)
FACT_DISPUTE: <fact id>   (optional, repeatable; challenge a fact)
